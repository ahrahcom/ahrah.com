Questions

What founders ask before they hire us.

What does Ahrah do?

Ahrah designs, builds, and runs software and AI products. Development, DevOps, testing, and project leadership are one practice, so you are not coordinating four vendors to ship one release.

Where does Ahrah work?

Ahrah works with companies worldwide, with a focus on the United States, the United Kingdom, Dubai and the wider UAE, and India. Delivery is remote-first. The working day is built around those markets, and active engagements include round-the-clock access to the lead.

Who will I actually work with?

You work with the people doing the work. Ahrah is led by founder Harsika, who has more than twelve years in software development, DevOps, project management, and testing. Early-stage work stays close to that leadership.

Can one engagement cover build, cloud, testing, and management?

Yes. That is the usual shape. The lead who agrees the scope stays accountable for the application, the pipeline, the tests, and the plan. Specialists are added when the work needs them, not as a handoff chain.

Are you available outside local business hours?

Yes. On an active engagement the accountable lead is reachable 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, across New York, London, Dubai, and India hours.

How fast can a new project start, and how do you bill?

Often within days of agreeing scope. Ahrah aims for a first usable slice in weeks. Billing follows a written scope and delivery phases, not an open-ended retainer.

Can you rescue a product that is already in trouble?

Yes. Ahrah stabilizes what is live, hardens the paths customers depend on, and ships the next version. A full rewrite is a decision, not the default.

Notes

Short answers for teams in the US, UK, Dubai, and India making the same decisions.

DELIVERY

What a first release should prove

A first release is the smallest product a real user can finish a real job with, on infrastructure you can deploy again tomorrow. If the customer cannot complete the core task, the release is not done. Everything else waits for the next cycle.

DEVOPS

DevOps before a platform team

You do not need a platform department to need DevOps. You need a repeatable way to build, ship, observe, and roll back: repository standards, CI, environments, infrastructure as code, and alerts that deserve to wake someone.

QUALITY

Testing that protects a launch

Testing is how a small team earns the right to move quickly. Tie it to the risks in the product: the payment, the permission, the migration, the integration that fails quietly. Automate what must not break, and explore what a script will miss.
