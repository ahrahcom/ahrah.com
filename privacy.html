2. Our role: controller and processor

When we decide why and how personal information is used, for example when you send us an enquiry or become a client, we act as a controller (called a "data fiduciary" under Indian law). This notice covers that information.

When we build or run software for a client, we may handle personal information that belongs to the client's own users. In that case we act as a processor (a "service provider" under US state laws, or a "data processor" under Indian law) and follow the client's written instructions and our agreement with them. If your information is in a system we operate for a client, please contact that client first; we will help them respond to you.

4. How we use it and our legal bases

We use personal information to answer enquiries, write proposals, deliver and support our services, send invoices, keep our systems secure, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations, and occasionally send you relevant updates about our work if you have agreed or the law allows it.

Where the EU GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Contract: to take steps you ask for before an engagement, and to perform the engagement.
  • Legitimate interests: to reply to business enquiries, run and secure our website and systems, and improve our services. We only rely on this where our interests are not overridden by yours.
  • Legal obligation: to keep accounting and tax records and respond to lawful requests.
  • Consent: for optional marketing emails. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Where India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies, we process your information based on your consent, which you give when you contact us for a stated purpose, or for the legitimate uses the Act allows, such as complying with law. We do not use your information for automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Cookies and tracking

Ahrah.com uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how people use the site. Google may set cookies and receive your IP address, browser details, and pages you view. We use this to measure traffic and improve the site. We do not use advertising pixels, session recording, or sell or share your information for targeted advertising. You can opt out with a browser "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal, or Google's analytics opt-out tools. Google's use of this information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, your browser sends your IP address and browser details to Google so the fonts can be delivered.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only when needed, with:

  • Service providers who help us run the business, such as email and office software, website hosting, cloud infrastructure, video calls, accounting, and payment processing. They may use the information only to provide their service to us.
  • Specialists and subcontractors who work on a client engagement under confidentiality obligations, only when the client has agreed.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, accountants, and auditors.
  • Authorities when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our clients, or the public.
  • A successor business if Ahrah is reorganised, merged, or sold, subject to this notice.

7. International transfers

Ahrah is based in India and works with clients worldwide, so your information may be handled in India and in other countries where we or our service providers operate. When we transfer personal information out of the UK, the EU, or the UAE, we use the safeguards those laws require, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or equivalent contractual protections. Transfers out of India follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and any country restrictions notified by the Government of India.

8. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement: up to 24 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Client and billing records: for the engagement and then for at least eight years, as Indian accounting and tax law requires.
  • Job applications: up to 12 months after the role is filled, unless you agree to a longer period.
  • Server logs: for the limited period set by our hosting provider.

When we no longer need information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

9. Security

We protect personal information with measures suited to the risk, including encryption in transit, multi-factor authentication on business accounts, least-privilege access, and secure handling of client credentials. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where the law requires it, including within 72 hours to UK or EU regulators and to India's Data Protection Board and CERT-In within the time limits Indian law sets.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information;
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including for direct marketing;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing; and
  • complain to a data protection authority.

We give these rights to everyone who asks, wherever you live, unless the law prevents it. To make a request, email business@ahrah.com with the subject "Privacy request". We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will reply within one month, or sooner where local law sets a shorter period. Making a request is free, and we will not treat you differently for using your rights. The sections below add the rules for specific regions.

12. United Kingdom and European residents

If you are in the United Kingdom, the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 apply. If you are in the European Economic Area or Switzerland, the EU GDPR or Swiss data protection law applies. Ahrah Technologies LLP is the controller of the information described in this notice. Our legal bases are listed in section 4, and your rights in section 10.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or to the data protection authority in the EU country where you live or work. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

13. United Arab Emirates residents

If you are in the UAE, we handle your information in line with Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data. If you or your company operate in the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) or the Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM), the DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020 or the ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021 may apply instead. You have the rights in section 10, including the right to complain to the relevant UAE authority, such as the UAE Data Office or the DIFC Commissioner of Data Protection.

  • correct, complete, update, or erase your personal data;
